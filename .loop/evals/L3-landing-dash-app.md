eval: L3-landing-dash-app

The `dash` Nomad job serves a config-driven (JSON) tile grid whose per-tile
status is computed live from `cli`'s existing `judge_all()`/`join()` logic,
sits entirely behind L1's already-deployed oauth2-proxy, never leaks a live
credential value to the browser, and reaches Nomad/Consul through a new
grant no broader than `read-job`/`list-jobs`/`node:read`.
