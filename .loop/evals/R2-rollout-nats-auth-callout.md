eval: R2-rollout-nats-auth-callout

**Definition of Done:** The NATS auth-callout bridge is deployed and the
`nats` server delegates authentication to it, so a client presenting a valid
Vault (human OIDC) or Nomad WI (machine) JWT is issued a scoped NATS user JWT
and can pub/sub only on its permitted subjects, while a non-permitted subject
is denied and an invalid or absent token is rejected fail-closed. All live
rows depend on **F1** (Nomad WI trust) and **F2** (Vault OIDC JWKS) being
landed; without their issuer/JWKS endpoints the callout has nothing to
validate against and these rows cannot pass.
