# Eval: U7 bump Bifrost to 2.0.0

eval: U7-upgrade-bifrost-2x

**Definition of Done:** the gateway runs 2.0.0 and accepts `documents` as a
list of bare strings on `/v1/rerank`, every other measured 1.6.7 behavior
still holds, `scripts/bifrost_smoke.py` proves the three auth behaviors
against BOTH versions, and `terraform plan` shows the bump and nothing else.

**Applies to every row:** the 1.6.7 baseline in the Expected column was
measured on 2026-09-05 before any edit, against
`http://192.168.2.50:8080`. A row that asserts an after-value without its
before-value having been captured is not scorable, because it cannot
separate "2.0.0 broke it" from "it was never true".

**Guardrail rows are not optional.** Two of the three smoke assertions are
security posture. A regression there is worse than a failed upgrade,
because the gateway stays up and serves while unprotected.
