# Decisions

Append-only records of choices that rejected a real alternative, never written over.

* [ADR 0001: Durable knowledge is kept in a validated OKF bundle, not in docs/ or AGENTS.md](0001-durable-knowledge-lives-in-an-okf-bundle.md) - Why this repository keeps its decisions, lessons and unbuilt proposals in .okf/ with checked frontmatter and an append-only decisions directory, rather than as more pages in docs/ or more paragraphs in AGENTS.md.
* [ADR 0002: docs/ is sorted by reader intent, and holds only pages for people who use the cluster](0002-docs-are-sorted-by-reader-intent.md) - docs/ gains how-to/, reference/ and explanation/, one folder per Diátaxis type. Mixed pages are split by section with their wording kept, and material for people working on the repository moves to .okf/.
* [ADR 0003: Lab names resolve from public DNS, not a local resolver](0003-lab-names-resolve-from-public-dns.md) - Names under lab.orangecluster.nl are public A records pointing at a private address, and the dnsmasq split-horizon resolver on firebat was removed. This trades a little disclosure for household DNS that does not depend on the cluster.
* [ADR 0004: The .consul zone is not forwarded to Consul](0004-consul-names-are-not-forwarded.md) - LAN clients cannot resolve .consul names, and that stays so. Forwarding the zone would hand out container bridge addresses that connect to nothing, so clients use node addresses and ports instead.
* [ADR 0005: Short-lived Postgres credentials come from Vault's database secrets engine, not PostgreSQL 18 native OAuth](0005-postgres-credentials-come-from-vaults-database-engine.md) - The S2 spike proved Path A, Vault minting a Postgres user per lease, against memex's real tables, and recommended R3 roll it out. Path B, PostgreSQL 18 OAuth, was rejected because no usable validator exists for Vault or Nomad tokens.
* [ADR 0006: Each job that mints database credentials gets its own Vault JWT role, and the shared nomad-workloads policy is not widened](0006-each-converted-job-gets-its-own-vault-jwt-role.md) - A job reading database/creds/* names a dedicated JWT role whose policy also carries its KV grants. Widening nomad-workloads was one line of Ansible but would let every workload mint database users.
* [ADR 0007: OpenViking's static MinIO key carries no claim-mode policy](0007-openviking-static-key-has-no-claim-policy.md) - OpenViking's S3 config cannot take short-lived credentials, so its job reads the openviking MinIO user's static key from Vault. No minio_iam_policy named openviking sits beside it, because one would read as live while granting nothing.
* [ADR 0008: OpenViking's rerank goes through Bifrost, not straight to embark](0008-openviking-rerank-goes-through-bifrost.md) - OpenViking reranks through Bifrost like every other model call, which meant upgrading Bifrost to 2.0.0 so it accepts bare-string documents. Pointing rerank at embark directly would have worked without the upgrade and was rejected.
* [ADR 0009: OpenViking has no browser surface; Web Studio is unmounted and its proxy removed](0009-openviking-has-no-browser-surface.md) - oauth2-proxy-openviking and Web Studio are gone because Studio cannot collect a credential under oidc. Studio is unmounted rather than left up without its proxy, and the hostname now serves ov-dash.
* [ADR 0010: Vault generates each person's TOTP QR, and self-enrollment stays off](0010-vault-generates-each-totp-qr.md) - Enrollment is an admin-generate run per person through just mfa_enroll. enable_self_enrollment was rejected because no Terraform provider exposes it and it guards a password stolen after enrollment only.
* [ADR 0011: Login MFA is enforced on the userpass mount accessor, not on an entity or a mount type](0011-login-mfa-enforcement-names-the-userpass-mount.md) - The TOTP login enforcement names vault_auth_backend.userpass.accessor. Naming the operator entity challenged the ov-dash trade on jwt-lab, and naming the mount type would have widened it to jwt-nomad.
* [ADR 0012: TOTP enrollment has no app on dash](0012-no-mfa-enrollment-app-on-dash.md) - Nobody builds an enrollment page behind SSO, because an unenrolled person cannot complete the Vault login that reaching dash requires. Vault's own UI is the GUI after enrollment.
